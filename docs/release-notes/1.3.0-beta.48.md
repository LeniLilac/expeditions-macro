# Expeditions Macro v1.3.0-beta.48

This beta hardens production detector ownership, adds a source-only Detector Viewer, improves Bounty Board reconciliation, and reuses a verified Team across routes within one Macro operation. It is published as a silent prerelease and does not send a Discord release announcement.

## Highlights

- Adds a standalone read-only Detector Viewer for images, recursive folders, and Deep Debug ZIPs. It overlays production geometry and live actions, explains emitted checks and thresholds, and labels details that production does not safely expose.
- Audits detector ownership across Settings, Events, Challenges, Teams, Refuel, Stage selection, chat, placement, Bounties, and Expeditions. Narrow borders use bounded local raster evidence with independent owner controls instead of one exact pixel row.
- Remembers the verified Team for one user-started Macro operation and one Roblox process, avoiding repeated Team switches when consecutive routes use the same slot.
- Derives usable Bounty slots from live yellow Reroll and green Claim controls, so a dimmed card may occupy any slot and never receives input.
- Corrects Mythic Bounty #2 to use Flower Forest wave 30.

## Bounty claim behavior

- A claim still requires the live green action and an action-anchored `#1` through `#10` identity before input.
- After reward dismissal, two fresh Board observations must agree that the slot settled dimmed or exposed a new yellow Reroll action.
- Both outcomes increment the daily claim count and clear the completed active progress.
- Only a dimmed slot removes that Bounty number from the reroll pool until UTC reset. A new yellow Reroll action keeps the number eligible.
- The beta.45 through beta.47 Bounty schema is reset rather than translating Bounty #2's incorrect Fairy King Forest progress key. The user confirmed that deployed Bounty progress does not require migration.

## Detector safety boundaries

- A one-pixel decorative line, isolated glyph edge, changing artwork, roster, reward, scene, or page-body brightness cannot own a state.
- Thin useful evidence is searched only inside a field-supported local band and remains subordinate to independent semantic or structural proof.
- OCR or template output may corroborate a crop only after another detector owns that bounded region. Text alone never owns a screen or click.
- The Detector Viewer is read-only and never changes detector decisions or owns Roblox input.
- Catalog discovery and its coverage audit are independent. The final audit covers 44 production decision owners through 52 catalog entries, including matchers, metrics, and scorers that do not use a Detector or Recognizer class suffix.
