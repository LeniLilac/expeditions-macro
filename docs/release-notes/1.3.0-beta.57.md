# Expeditions Macro v1.3.0-beta.57

This beta prevents Bounty slot starvation and unbounded Gold spending, while hardening two field-observed visual states. It is published as a silent prerelease and does not send a Discord release announcement.

## Highlights

- Processes live Bounty slots in bounded round-robin order, so a viable Mythic in physical slot five is observed before an unresolved earlier slot can retry.
- Persists a fixed 750-paid-reroll safety limit for each UTC day and sends no 751st reroll.
- Recognizes the supported high-contrast in-match Return to Lobby door without depending on a brittle one-pixel count ceiling.
- Recognizes a completed leftmost Bounty `#10` whose paper suffix is shifted by the supplied raster phase.

## Safety boundaries

- Every Bounty slot retains its own evidence tracker and 400-cycle ceiling; the complete board transaction also has a 2,000-cycle hard ceiling.
- A paid reroll is counted only after fresh, stable Board settlement. No-Gold, unchanged, unowned, and unexpected settlements do not increment the counter.
- Every counted reroll is atomically persisted before another paid input, even if cancellation arrives immediately afterward.
- Reaching 750 preserves active Bounty progress, permits already-active routes and other Macro Plan tasks to continue, and waits until the next applicable active-work reset or midnight UTC before new Bounty work.
- Existing schema-2 Bounty claims, exclusions, and objective progress remain intact when the new reroll counter is absent.
- Door ownership still requires independent top edge, jamb, open interior, handle, arrow, fill, and local-contrast evidence.
- The clipped `#10` allowance is limited to a live Claim action at the reviewed leftmost right-scrolled card; similarity and runner-up separation remain mandatory.
