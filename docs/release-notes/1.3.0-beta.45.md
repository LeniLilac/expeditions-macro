# Expeditions Macro v1.3.0-beta.45

This beta adds deterministic Mythic Bounty automation. It is published as a silent prerelease and does not send a Discord release announcement.

## Highlights

- Adds **Bounty** as a seventh Macro Plan task mode in a two-row selector.
- Opens the Event Bounty Board, scans all five cards, claims completed Mythics, and identifies `#1` through `#10` from suffix-only card evidence.
- Routes the known Raid Act 1, Story Infinite, and Challenge objectives, merging overlapping objectives into the most efficient available match.
- Supports a zero-through-four non-viable parking policy: zero spends more reroll Gold for faster parallel work; four saves Gold by retaining more skipped Mythics.
- Keeps conditional Challenge Bounties through ordinary cooldowns and rerolls them after the daily Challenge limit.
- Stops rerolling on the explicit insufficient-Gold banner, with bounded attempt-based fallbacks, finishes active viable work, and retries only on the next Macro start.
- Persists active Bounty progress locally across stop/start and UTC resets for the one Roblox account assumed per Windows user.
- Blocks Bounty authoring until Spirit City Raid Act 1 and all five Story Infinite Placement Setups are configured.
- Continues partial objectives directly from the verified Lobby and reopens the board only when a whole active Bounty is ready to claim.
- Maps a completed card's left-shifted green Claim action back to its owning Bounty while preserving the live click center.
- Accepts densely populated Unit Inventories without allowing changing bright roster artwork to suppress the verified Teams action.
- Invalidates the previous match's Fast camera-preparation cache after every Infinite-to-Lobby return.
- Migrates upgrading users once to the field-confirmed Gold Mine and Resource Drill route defaults while preserving later edits.

## Safety boundaries

- Every board input requires fresh owner-state and live action geometry.
- Yellow rerolls retain a safe 200-millisecond response interval.
- Number classification uses only the anchored numeric suffix, not quest order, title text, stars, or paper artwork.
- Infinite objectives leave at target wave plus two. A bounded increasing-wave fallback requires three strictly increasing later readings, ignores duplicates, and resets on unknown, low, or regressing evidence.
- Local Bounty progress is not included in plan share codes.
