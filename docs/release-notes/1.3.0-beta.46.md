# Expeditions Macro v1.3.0-beta.46

This beta hardens Bounty Challenge handoff and saved-team confirmation. It is published as a silent prerelease and does not send a Discord release announcement.

## Highlights

- Returns from Bounty Challenge inspection through the verified Play Back layers and in-match Lobby door instead of asserting Lobby while the match is still active.
- Finishes every currently executable active Bounty objective before reopening the board to claim or reroll.
- Resumes persisted active Bounty work after process recovery without an unnecessary board visit.
- Accepts the field-observed Team 1 Load Team confirmation over a bright populated roster while still requiring visible Load Team rows, modal structure, and the live Confirm action.

## Safety boundaries

- Every Play Back, Lobby door, confirmation, and Events click remains authorized by fresh owned-state evidence.
- Saved-team confirmation still requires at least two visible Load Team rows, the dimmed modal structure, and an independently detected live Confirm action.
- Unknown or unsettled Bounty and saved-team states continue to fail closed without fallback coordinate input.
