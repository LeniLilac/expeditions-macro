# Expeditions Macro v1.3.0-beta.53

This beta keeps long Deep Debug operations practical, adds deterministic Mythic Bounty #10 routing, and hardens three field-sensitive detectors. It is published as a silent prerelease and does not send a Discord release announcement.

## Highlights

- Deep Debug keeps the complete operation event trace and sanitized text log while retaining only the final configurable 1–120 minutes of PNG frames; the default is 15 minutes.
- Deep Debug manifests expose total, retained, and discarded frame counts, and both diagnostic viewers understand intentionally pruned frames without collapsing the full timeline.
- Mythic Bounty #10 now completes five Challenges, School Grounds Story Act 1 on Hard, and School Grounds Infinite through wave 15.
- The reviewed post-Cancel Bounty `#9`, red-scene Areas menu, and bright-blue Units scrollbar variants are recognized without weakening their independent state-ownership requirements.

## Safety boundaries

- Frame retention removes only old PNG captures; complete structured events and sanitized text logs remain available for the whole operation.
- Bounty suffix variants still require a live card action, bounded title geometry, high similarity, and a clear runner-up margin before any input is authorized.
- Areas and Units variants remain gated by their complete owning-page structure and full cross-state negative coverage.
- Bounty #10 follows the existing Challenge cooldown versus daily-limit policy and reuses verified Story and Infinite workflows.

- 1,518 non-golden application tests passed.
