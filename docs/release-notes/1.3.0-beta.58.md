# Expeditions Macro v1.3.0-beta.58

This beta makes saved-team loading reliable during active matches and removes unnecessary Team-list scrolling. It is published as a silent prerelease and does not send a Discord release announcement.

## Highlights

- Recognizes the real Unit Teams Close control and scrollbar when the in-match Stage control is visible beside the panel.
- Loads Team 2 directly from the fully visible second row without scrolling.
- Uses fully visible Team 4 and Team 5 rows after the first accepted drag instead of waiting through redundant correction cycles.
- Preserves operation-scoped Team reuse, so a positively loaded Team is still reused only within the same user-started Macro and Roblox process.

## Safety boundaries

- Unit Teams ownership still requires the bounded red Close control, at least two complete green Load Team rows, and the height-bounded scrollbar to agree.
- A requested Team action is accepted only from a complete visible button on two fresh observations; clipped rows and unrelated red controls cannot authorize input.
- Scrollbar drags remain acknowledged, deadline-bounded, and capped at five alignment attempts. The settled result of the final allowed drag is still inspected.
- Unit Inventory retains its independent Close, Unequip All, Teams, and Quick Sell control proof before the Team list can open.

## Validation

- Replayed representative frames from all 13 supplied Team runs; every frame now resolves to the correct owned Team workflow state, including all four in-match failures.
- Added a canonical privacy-reviewed in-match Team-list fixture retaining the competing Stage control, plus direct Team 2, Team 4/5 under-travel, clipped-row, slow-observation, final-drag, and cross-state negative coverage.
- Passed 1,635 non-golden application tests and 120 golden-image and cross-state detector tests.
- Passed 6 Deep Debug Viewer tests, 15 Detector Viewer tests, and all 90 dark/light main-app UI states.
- Passed locked restore, zero-warning Release build, repository policy, scoped formatting, diff hygiene, packaging, and privacy checks.
